Q: When did Apollo 11 land on the Moon, and who first stepped onto the lunar surface?
EXPECTED: Apollo 11 landed on the Moon on July 20, 1969. Neil Armstrong was the first person to step onto the lunar surface, followed by Buzz Aldrin.

CATEGORY: emergency
MISSION: apollo_13
Q: What major emergency occurred during the Apollo 13 mission?
EXPECTED: Apollo 13 experienced an oxygen tank explosion in the service module, which caused loss of power and oxygen and forced the crew to abort the lunar landing and return to Earth using the lunar module as a lifeboat.
---

CATEGORY: technical
MISSION: apollo_13
Q: How did the Apollo 13 crew and Mission Control conserve power and keep the spacecraft habitable after the accident?
EXPECTED: After the explosion, the crew powered down the command module and used the lunar module for life support and propulsion, carefully managing power, water, and lithium hydroxide canisters for CO2 removal under guidance from Mission Control.

CATEGORY: disaster analysis
MISSION: challenger
Q: What caused the Space Shuttle Challenger disaster, and when did it occur?
EXPECTED: The Challenger (STS-51-L) disaster occurred on January 28, 1986, when the shuttle broke apart shortly after launch due to failure of an O-ring seal in a solid rocket booster, exacerbated by cold weather at launch.
